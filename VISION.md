# Vision

## Why Navo Exists

Navo exists to build software that works the way people actually plan and move through their days.

People do not experience life as a list of tasks to tick off. They form intentions, carry context, discover what needs to happen, and then execute it all while moving through the real physical world — between home, office, shops, schools, and everywhere in between.

Today's productivity tools reduce this to lists, boards, and calendars. These are useful in parts. But they miss the most important dimension of how real work gets done: **you are always somewhere, heading somewhere, and that determines everything**.

Navo is built to model that reality.

---

## The Problem

Most planning software asks you to define your day in the abstract.

Write the list. Set the time. Pick the date.

But real life doesn't work like that.

You start the day with a vague intention: "get vegetables for dinner." You don't yet know which vegetables, which shop, or when. As the day unfolds, you discover that you're passing a farmers' market on your way to the office. That moment — being in the right place at the right time — is what actually determines whether the work gets done.

Traditional tools have no concept of this. They cannot see your route. They cannot surface the opportunity. They cannot tell you that two errands share a location and can be combined into one stop.

The gap between "the plan" and "what actually happens" exists because software ignores physical reality.

---

## Our Belief

**Planning is navigation.**

Not just navigation through roads and streets, but navigation through the day itself — through work, time, location, decisions, and opportunities.

Every person has a position: where they are right now, where they're going, and what they carry with them (budget, time, needs, context).

Every intention has multiple possible paths. Some paths are more efficient. Some are only possible if you're already nearby. Some open up because of where you're headed.

The goal of planning is not to produce a perfect schedule.

The goal is to continuously surface the best next action given where you are, what you have, and what you're trying to achieve.

---

## The Core Insight

When you plan your day, your route through locations is the most important variable.

As you move:
- Some work becomes possible (you pass the right shop)
- Some work becomes impossible (you miss the window)
- New opportunities emerge (you're near somewhere relevant you hadn't considered)

This is **opportunity-aware planning**. The route enables and disables work in real time.

Navo is the first planning system built around this reality.

---

## Our Vision

Navo is a day planning system grounded in the physical world.

**Intents** form with context before the plan exists.

**Work** is discovered progressively as the intent becomes clearer.

**Locations** are where work happens — first-class entities, not tags.

**Routes** connect locations and determine what is possible each day.

**Opportunities** surface when location, time, and available work align.

**The map** is the primary interface through which all of this becomes real.

The plan adapts continuously as the day unfolds.

---

## What This Looks Like

You wake up with the intent: "get vegetables for dinner, cooking for 4, budget ₹400."

You haven't planned where yet. That's fine. The intent is captured.

As you plan your day — home to office to gym — the map shows you that a fresh produce market is 200m off your route to the office. Given your budget and the time window, stopping there works.

The opportunity surfaces. You act on it. The intent is fulfilled.

No rigid scheduling. No manually hunting for the right shop. No separate grocery list app.

One system. Intent → Work → Location → Route → Opportunity → Done.

---

## Core Principles

- Intent comes before the plan.
- Context is present from the moment intent forms.
- Work is discovered progressively, not defined upfront.
- Location and route determine what is possible.
- The map is the foundation, not a feature.
- Opportunities are the product — surfaced at the right moment.
- Planning is continuous adaptation, not a one-time event.
- Software adapts to people, not the other way around.

---

## Long-Term Direction

Our long-term vision is a planning platform where intents, work, context, locations, routes, and opportunities exist in one connected system.

Whether you are planning a single day, a week of errands, a vacation, or a complex project — Navo provides the same experience:

> Understand where you are. Understand what matters. Help you move forward with clarity.

The map and the route are always at the centre.

# Planning

Planning in Navo is not a phase. It is a continuous activity that runs throughout the day.

---

## What Planning Means in Navo

Traditional planning asks you to define everything upfront: make a list, set times, assign priorities.

Navo's planning model is different.

**Planning in Navo means:** given what you want to achieve, where you are, where you're going, and what time you have — what is the best sequence of actions for today?

It is a continuous process of discovery, decision, and adaptation — not a one-time event.

---

## The Day as the Unit of Planning

The primary planning horizon in Navo is the day.

A day plan answers:

- What intents am I working on today?
- What work do those intents require?
- Where does that work happen?
- What is my route through those locations?
- What opportunities arise on that route?
- What is the most efficient sequence?

This is fundamentally different from a to-do list. A to-do list ignores location, route, and opportunity. A Navo day plan is grounded in physical reality.

---

## Planning Is Route-First

The most important decision in planning a day is the **route** — the sequence of locations you will move through.

The route determines everything:

- Which work is accessible without a detour
- Which shops or places can be reached within time windows
- Which opportunities naturally arise as you move

Planning begins with understanding the day's geography, then fitting work into it.

A piece of work with a location becomes plannable only when it connects to the route.

---

## Planning Is Continuous

Plans are not fixed. They evolve as:

- Work is completed (unlocking next steps)
- Context changes (location, time, energy)
- New opportunities appear (something becomes possible mid-route)
- Something unexpected happens (a shop is closed, a meeting runs over)

Navo continuously reconsiders the plan in light of current reality. It does not hold on to a plan that no longer fits.

---

## Planning vs. Scheduling vs. Routing

These are three distinct activities in Navo:

| Activity       | Question it answers                                      |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Planning**   | What needs to happen and in what order?                  |
| **Scheduling** | When exactly should this happen?                         |
| **Routing**    | Where does it happen and how do I get there efficiently? |

Most tools conflate these. Navo keeps them separate because they evolve at different speeds and serve different purposes.

Not all work needs to be scheduled. Some work happens opportunistically when the context is right.

---

## The Planning Loop

```
Active Intents
      ↓
Discover Work needed today
      ↓
Identify Locations for each piece of work
      ↓
Plan the Route connecting those locations
      ↓
Surface Opportunities along the route
      ↓
Execute work as you move
      ↓
Update plan as context changes
      ↓
Repeat
```

This loop runs continuously throughout the day.

---

## What Planning Is Not

- Planning is not writing a complete task list before starting
- Planning is not assigning rigid time slots to every action
- Planning is not a one-time decision made in the morning
- Planning is not separate from the physical world you move through

Planning in Navo is the continuous act of asking: _given where I am and what I want, what is the best thing to do next?_
